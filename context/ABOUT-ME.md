# About Myself

My name is Konstantin Gredeskoul, and I've been writing software for 30 years. I graduated with distinctions from Monash University, Australia, and went straight into programming. I self-learned C language on a project that upgraded Australia's National Rail Corporation's carriage tracking using sensors and a distributed network spannign the entire continent. I learned to use BEA Tuxedo, an ancient pre-decessor to WebLogic, and now likely entirely forgotten but amazing technology.

I came to the US in 1998 during the first Internet Boom and joined the tiny startup Topica that were building free email groups project. I stayed there for 7 years, got my green card, became a US citizen, and as a systems architect oversaw the company technology grow to deliver 1/4M messages per day around 2002.

After that we built the first proper email marketing platform with click and open tracking, and follow up campaigns, but nobody in 2004 could understand why would you need that? The company pivoted to sending spam on behalf of brokers who paid large checks, and got blocked by all major providers, and thus shot itself in the foot.

By that time I was already writing Java for a security company Red Seal Systems, with a strong team, but I didn't not like the commute to San Mateo, so after six months I switched to Blurb.com where with four otther engineers we learnt Ruby and Rails pre-1.0 and built a full e-commerce marketplace in less than 4 months, which included migration from a PHP-based storefront. I was blown away and instantly in love with a language that was so compact, and the framework that used convention over configuration (the exact opposite of Hibernate ORM which I hate).
