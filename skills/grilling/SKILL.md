---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a spec, plan or implementation. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. 

Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. 

For each question, provide your recommended answer or two with reasoning and allow me to quickly select one of the options if I like it.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. 

Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering and confusing.

Keep questions concise, and do not make any assumptions other than whatever may have already been done in this project.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the codebase, or looking up in the previous specifications, look it up rather than asking me. 

The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not enact the final plan (plan.md) until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.

We can decide that some of the questions or features are to be deferred to a later day. In this case you will create a separate folder (see skill ''create-a-plan") to store this information in the follow-up folder.
